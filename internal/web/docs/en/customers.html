Customers and packages

Packages

A package holds the limits a customer gets: how many domains, mailboxes and aliases, how much storage all mailboxes may use together, the size of a new mailbox and the largest one, and the sending limits. Billing systems refer to a package by its short name (for example business-10).

  • Messages per hour / per day (per mailbox) and per hour for the whole customer protect the reputation of the server's address: a mailbox whose password was stolen cannot send thousands of spam messages. Messages over the limit wait in the queue.
  • Recipients per message stops one message from going to thousands of addresses.
  • Changing a customer to a smaller package deletes nothing; nothing new can be added above the new limits.

Customers

A customer owns domains and mailboxes. Every customer's mail is stored under its own system user, so even a fault in the mail software cannot let one customer read another customer's mail. A customer can never route mail into another customer's mailboxes, send as their addresses or see their domains.

  • Panel logins: the customer signs in to the customer panel at the same address as the admin panel. Every login must set up two-factor sign-in at the first sign-in.
  • Billing ID: the customer's number in your billing system; the REST API finds customers by it.

Suspend and terminate

  • Suspended: mail for the customer's domains is still received (nothing is lost), but nobody of the customer can sign in, use mail programs or send. Sessions end at once.
  • Terminated: mail for the customer's domains is refused. The data stays until it is deleted.
  • Activate brings a suspended or terminated customer back.
